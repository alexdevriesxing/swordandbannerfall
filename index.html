Choose your match

Press P during preparation to switch between VS AI and Local 2P. The two champions each have nine AI-controlled allies. Press Enter to begin.

Standard gamepad

Controller 1 controls Blue. In Local 2P, Controller 2 controls Red. You can mix controllers and keyboard.
